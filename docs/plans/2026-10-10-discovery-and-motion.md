Make the live project understandable to search engines and AI retrieval systems, and deliver two editable, playable motion drafts explaining its single purpose.

- Canonical origin: https://opencoast.kaelux.dev, verified live on 10 October 2026.
- Add human-readable static HTML pages about the project, contribution flow and evidence labels; link them from the map.

- Use slow, predictable transitions and readable holds. Deliver two light silent drafts, editable source and a screen-recording shot list. No voice-generation account or paid service required.
