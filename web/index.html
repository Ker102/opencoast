Know your coast.

OpenCoast is a map of beach and coastal access information. See access areas, routes to shore, local conditions and the sources behind a claim. Browse and contribute without an account.

Explore the map ↗

Understand where you can go

Coastal access rights vary by location. A beach, the land beside it and the path reaching it can have different rules. OpenCoast brings those claims and their evidence onto one map, with allowed, conditional, restricted, disputed and unknown access labels.

Read the evidence behind the map

Moderators review every change before it appears. Document-backed records identify official sources checked against the claim. Community-reviewed records without a verified official source carry a separate label. Unmarked places have no reviewed information yet.

Help people know their coastal access rights

Draw an area, route or point, explain the local conditions and add a source when you can. Official legal documents are strongly encouraged. You can suggest a correction anonymously, and keep a private receipt to follow its review.
